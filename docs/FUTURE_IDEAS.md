# Future Ideas Backlog

> [!NOTE]  
> This document is a backlog of experimental ideas and "nice-to-have" features. These are **not** currently slated for development and should not be implemented until they are moved to `ROADMAP.md`.

## AI Enhancements
- **Predictive Prep List**: The system analyzes past sales data, weather patterns, and local events to automatically generate a prep list ("Prep 40 portions of salmon tonight").
- **Conversational POS**: A voice-activated interface for servers to quickly ring in complex orders without touching a screen ("Add a table 4: two steaks, medium rare, one with no asparagus").
- **Dynamic Pricing**: (Experimental) Suggesting happy hour pricing dynamically based on real-time slow periods.

## Operational Integrations
- **Augmented Reality Table Mapping**: Using LiDAR on an iPad to map the physical layout of the restaurant into the system instantly.
- **Automated 86ing**: Integrating smart scales under kegs or inventory shelves to automatically 86 (remove from menu) items the second they run out physically.
- **Wearable Integration**: Pushing discrete haptic notifications to servers' Apple Watches when a VIP guest sits at their table or their food is ready in the window.

## Guest Experience
- **Invisible Payment**: Uber-style payments where a guest can simply walk out after their meal, and the card on file for their reservation is charged automatically.
- **Taste Profile Sync**: Remembering a guest's preference (e.g., "always orders sparkling water, allergic to shellfish") across different restaurants using RestaurantOS.
